Common Questions

Do you do commercial work?

Yes. Alongside home remodeling we do commercial and office build-outs - restaurants, medical and dental offices, studios, and retail. We can work around your operating hours so you stay open, or run straight through to hit an opening date.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Rogers Park and work across Chicago's North and Northwest Side and the northern suburbs — including Edgewater, Andersonville, West Ridge, Lincoln Square, Evanston, Skokie, and Wilmette.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Paro Projects is a licensed and insured general contractor. We pull permits and coordinate every trade on your project.

Do you take on small projects?

Yes. We are happy to talk about a single built-in, a repair, or one room. No project is too small to ask about.

Do you work on old houses?

It is most of what we do. We restored our own 1890s Chicago house, and we specialize in renovating century-old homes without stripping out their character.

How do I get started?

Call us at (312) 545-4924 or fill out the form on our contact page. We follow up within two business days to talk through what you need.
