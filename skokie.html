The houses here

Skokie is dominated by 1950s–60s brick ranches, split-levels, and Georgians on generous lots. The bones are usually sound; what dates them is closed-off floor plans, low-spec kitchens and baths, and unfinished basements.

What we do most in Skokie

  • Opening up ranch and split-level floor plans
  • Kitchen remodels and load-bearing wall removals
  • Primary suite and bathroom additions
  • Basement finishing and in-law suites
  • Backyard studios, saunas, and detached structures

Bigger lots in Skokie make backyard builds — ADUs, studios, saunas — much more practical than in the city.
